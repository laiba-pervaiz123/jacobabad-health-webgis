HOW IT WORKS

Database. API. Map.

About The Study Area

Jacobabad, Sindh

Jacobabad is a city and district headquarters in northern Sindh, Pakistan, close to the Balochistan border. It was founded in the 1840s and named after Brigadier General John Jacob.

The city is well known for its very hot summers. Easy access to healthcare is important here, so this map shows hospitals, medical centers, clinics, diagnostic labs, pharmacies and medical stores in and around the city.

The data was collected from Google Maps and field checking, stored in PostgreSQL / PostGIS, and published through an API.
